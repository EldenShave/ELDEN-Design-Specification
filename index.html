Product Design Specification V1.0

ELDEN Premium
Travel Trimmer

A monolithic, minimalist grooming device designed for modern travelers and professionals.

Reusing your proven internal platform with our exclusive industrial design.

01 — Product Overview

What We're Building

Core Concept

A premium grooming trimmer that doesn't look like a generic Alibaba product. The design is intentionally monolithic—a perfect cylinder with no unnecessary elements, no gaming aesthetics, no visible screws.

When holding the product, customers should immediately feel quality. The design remains timeless for many years: minimal, elegant, modern, professional.

Manufacturing Strategy

We are not reinventing internal electronics. Our goal is to reuse your existing and proven internal platform (motor, blade system, battery, PCB, charging electronics, waterproof sealing, internal frame, certifications).

The primary customization focuses on external housing, industrial design, surface finish, button design, charging dock, branding, and packaging.

02 — Design Philosophy

Premium, Minimal, Timeless

What We Don't Want

  • A generic OEM trimmer with a logo slapped on it.
  • Flat sides or ergonomic grips.
  • Visible screws or decorative elements.
  • Anything that looks dated in 5-10 years.

What We Want

  • Customers immediately recognize it as an ELDEN product.
  • Premium feel in hand. Dense, well-built, never hollow.
  • Minimal, elegant, modern, professional design.
  • Timeless design that looks modern 10 years from now.

Shape & Form

  • Perfect cylinder. No flat side. No ergonomic indentation.
  • No finger grooves. No asymmetrical body.
  • The cylindrical shape is one of the defining elements.
  • Extremely clean and minimalist appearance.

Materials & Finish

  • Housing: Premium engineering plastic (PC, PC+ABS, or equivalent).
  • Finish: Titanium gray, matte, brushed-metal visual effect.
  • Feel: Soft-touch, scratch resistant, fingerprint resistant if possible.
  • Weight: Dense and premium. Internal balancing weights if needed.

04 — Controls & Features

Interface Elements

Power Button

  • Circular. Centered vertically on the front face.
  • Slightly concave. Finger naturally rests inside.
  • Firm tactile click. Short travel.
  • Difficult to press accidentally in luggage or travel bag.

LED Indicator

  • Single discreet white LED positioned below the button.
  • Used for charging, battery level, power status.
  • Minimal and elegant appearance.

Branding

  • ELDEN logo. Laser engraving preferred.
  • Vertical orientation. Centered.
  • Approximately 20 mm above the lower ring.
  • No oversized branding. Premium and discreet.

05 — Charging & Waterproofing

Power & Durability

USB-C Charging

  • USB-C connector located underneath the trimmer.
  • Connector not visible during normal use.
  • No side or rear charging ports.
  • Charging occurs only through the bottom.

Waterproofing

  • One of the highest priorities.
  • Product should ideally support wet use.
  • Target: Suitable for shaving under the shower.
  • Preferred: IPX7 or equivalent using existing platform.

06 — Blade System & Interchangeable Heads

Two Core Heads + Future Expansion

Head 1: Precision Blade Head

  • Fine, professional T-Blade for complete shaving.
  • Designed for clean, precise grooming.
  • Perfect for facial shaving and detailed work.
  • High precision. Easy replacement.
  • Included in first production.

Head 2: Clipper Head with Guard System

  • Larger head designed to accommodate clipper guards.
  • Supports multiple guard sizes for beard maintenance.
  • Allows precise length control for beard grooming.
  • Compatible with standard clipper guard attachments.
  • Included in first production.

Removable Head Design

  • Both heads are entirely removable and interchangeable.
  • Separation point at upper circular joint.
  • Each head module detaches as one complete piece.
  • Simple, tool-free attachment and removal.
  • Allows users to switch between precision shaving and beard maintenance seamlessly.

Future Interchangeable Heads (Expansion Phase)

Beyond the two core heads included in first production, we envision expanding the ELDEN ecosystem with additional specialized heads. This future compatibility is important for the long-term development of the ELDEN brand.

Possible future accessories include:

  • Nose trimmer head
  • Wider body grooming head
  • Precision detailing head
  • Advanced beard styling head
  • Other specialized grooming attachments

07 — Premium Charging Dock

Vertical Charging Station

Dock Specifications

  • Diameter: 100–130 mm
  • Height: 25–35 mm
  • Heavy enough to remain stable on bathroom counter.
  • Circular. Minimal. Matching the trimmer aesthetic.

Charging Method

  • Recessed circular cavity in center of dock.
  • USB-C male connector rises from center.
  • Trimmer placed vertically into dock.
  • USB-C connector aligns naturally.

Dock LED Indicator

  • Single discreet LED visible from the front.
  • Green: Fully charged
  • Amber: Charging
  • Red: Low battery / charging error
  • Minimal brightness. Premium appearance.

11 — Questions for Manufacturers

What We Need From You

  • Which existing platform could support this design? What are the internal dimensions and capabilities?
  • Can the removable head system be integrated for both the precision blade head and the clipper head with guard system? Separation at upper circular joint, each head module detaches as one piece.
  • Estimated tooling cost for external housing (cylindrical casing, button, dock) and two interchangeable head modules (precision blade head and clipper head with guard system).
  • Estimated unit price for: 500 units, 1,000 units, 2,000 units. (Note: First production includes both precision blade head and clipper head with guard system.)
  • Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for initial production.
  • Development timeline from order to first samples to full production.
  • Waterproof options — Can you achieve IPX7 with your existing platform?
  • Surface finish options — What finishes are available? Can you achieve matte titanium gray?
  • Available engineering plastics — PC, PC+ABS, or other premium materials?
  • Existing certifications — CE, FCC, RoHS, etc.
  • Warranty policy — What warranty terms do you offer?
  • Spare parts availability — How quickly can you supply replacement parts?
  • Packaging customization — Can you customize the box, foam insert, and accessories packaging?
  • Ability to manufacture both head modules (precision blade head and clipper head with guard system) in-house or recommendation of partner suppliers.
  • Availability of clipper guard attachments compatible with the clipper head system.
  • Ability to manufacture the charging dock in-house or recommendation of partner supplier.
  • Ability to manufacture the PU leather travel case, or recommendation of a partner supplier if not.
